If $1 \leq i \leq k$, there are $n_{i}$ choices for the $ith$ slot, regardless of the previous choices

$$
Arrangements = n_{1}n_{2}\dots n_{k}
$$
Same idea as $|A \times B| = |A||B|$ for the [[Set Theory#^888b0e|Cartesian product]]

# Examples
- label = 1 letter + 3 digits: $26 \times 10^3 = 26000$; with no repeats: $26 \times 10 \times 9 \times 8 = 18720$
- 4-letter words with exactly one E: choose E's position, then the other 3 letters: $4 \times 25^3$

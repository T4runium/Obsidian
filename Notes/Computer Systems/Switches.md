Binary can be represented as high and low voltage (1 is high and 0 is low)
Normal electrical switches CAN be used but are extremely extremely slow and impractical

Switches using high and low because on and off require perfect precision which may not happen due to switch degradation and temperature interference 

**Relay switches**: 
- A mechanical contact pulled shut by an electro-magnet
- audible, slow and wears down fast

**Vacuum Tubes**:
- A heated filament controlling electrons across a vacuum
- No moving parts so it is faster
- It glows so the filament burns out
- Consumes a lot of power

**MOSFETS** (Metal Oxide Semiconductor Field Effect Transistor):
- A switch made of silicon with no moving parts and nothing to wear out
- Extremely small so are densely packed
- Very fast, low power

Silicon is not a good conductor of electricity but its cheap and abundant.
It is chemically manipulated to make it less pure resulting in better electrical conduction

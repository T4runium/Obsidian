Vector is an element of a vector space

**Vector space:**
consists of:
 - A set V of vectors
 - A [[Fields|field]] F of scalars
 - Vector addition on V
 - Scalar multiplication
 $$
 \therefore F.V = V
 $$
**Algebraic structure:**
- V forms a [[Groups|group]] under addition
- F forms a [[Fields|field]]
- Scalar multiplication links both sets together

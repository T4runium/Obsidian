pA group is a set G together with a [[Binary Operations|Binary Operation]] $*$ such that
- **Closure:** If $a, b \in G$ then $a * b \in G$
- **Associativity:** $(a * b) * c = a * (b * c)$ for all $a, b, c, \in G$  ^27c6ec
- **Identity:** There exists an $e \in G$ such that $a * e = e * a = a$ for all $a \in G$ ^5a8c2e
- **Inverse:** For every $a \in G$ there exists $a^{-1} \in G$ such that $a * a^{-1} = a^{-1} * a = e$ ^41920a

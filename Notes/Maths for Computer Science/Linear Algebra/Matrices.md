Number of Multiplications while multiplying 2 $n \times n$ matrices is $n^3$ ($n^2$ entries, each a [[Dot product]] of a row and a column with $n$ multiplications)
number of additions is $n^2(n - 1)$


$\omega$ —the smallest real number such that two n × n matrices can be multiplied in $O(n^\omega)$ time. (Currently, ω ≈ 2.371).

# Transpose
changing an $n \times m$ matrix into an $m \times n$ matrix
$$
\begin{aligned}
(A^T)^T &= A \\
(A \pm B)^T &= A^T \pm B^T \\
(\alpha A)^T &= \alpha A^T \\
(AB)^T &= B^TA^T
\end{aligned}
$$
# Symmetric Matrices
Symmetry along the diagonal: $A^T = A$
